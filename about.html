About Jamia Usmania Sabera Mission

Knowledge · Character · Service — since 2001

Our History

From a Single Classroom to a Thriving Jamia

Jamia Usmania Sabera Mission was founded in 2001 by a small group of well-wishers who saw the need for affordable, quality Islamic education in the locality. It began with 12 students and one teacher in a rented room.

By the grace of Allah and the support of the community, the jamia has grown steadily — adding a purpose-built campus, a masjid, a hostel, and a modern schooling wing — while keeping fees within reach of every family. Hundreds of our alumni today serve as huffaz, imams, teachers and responsible professionals.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To nurture knowledgeable, sincere and responsible citizens through combined Islamic and modern education, rooted in the Quran and Sunnah, regardless of a family's financial situation.

Our Vision

A generation of huffaz, ulama and professionals who lead their communities with ilm, akhlaq and khidmat — carrying the light of deeni knowledge into every walk of life.
