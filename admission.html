What happens next?
1. Our admission office will call you within 2 working days.
2. Visit the campus with the required documents for a short entrance test (Quran reading level).
3. On selection, complete the admission form at the office and pay the one-time admission fee.

Admission Process

  1. Submit this online enquiry form
  2. Receive a call from the admission office
  3. Visit campus with documents & entrance test
  4. Selection & fee payment at the office
  5. Welcome to the Jamia family!

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card of the student (copy)
  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school / madrasa report card
  • Transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • Guardian's ID proof (for hostel)

Important Dates

  • Application review: every Tuesday & Friday
  • Entrance tests: Sundays, 10:00 am
  • Session begins: 5 April 2027 (1368 AH)
Hostel is available for boys above 8 years. For girls' admission, please contact the office directly — the girls' section has a separate campus and staff.
