About the Jamia

Serving the Community Since 2001

Jamia Usmania Sabera Mission began with a handful of students under one teacher. Today, alhamdulillah, it educates hundreds of students in Islamic sciences and modern subjects under one roof.

  • Combined deeni and modern education from Class KG onwards
  • Separate, supervised arrangement for girls' education
  • Clean hostel with warden care and home-style meals
  • Monthly progress reports shared with parents
